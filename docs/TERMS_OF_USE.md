**Version:** 2026-11-draft-1. **Status:** draft for counsel review. This is not legal advice and must not be published until counsel approves it (PRD section 7).

## 1. The service

The service generates synthetic, simulated, CCTV-style video clips and related annotations ("Outputs") from text prompts and parameters. Every Output is artificially generated. None depicts real people, real places or real events.

## 2. Who may use it

The service is only for verified business customers who have completed our know-your-customer checks. Accounts may not be shared, resold or used by anonymous parties. You are responsible for every use made under your API keys.

## 3. Permitted uses

* Training and evaluating video-analytics and computer-vision systems, including rare-event detection.
* Testing analytics across controlled conditions.
* Building privacy-safe datasets.
* Training operators and responders with simulated incidents.
* Sales demonstrations by integrators, where the footage is clearly presented as synthetic.
* **Restricted:** public-safety operator training and drills. This requires an enterprise contract that names this use.

## 4. Prohibited uses

You must not, and must not allow anyone else to:

1. Use or present any Output as evidence. This covers investigations, complaints, court, arbitration, insurance claims and disciplinary proceedings. It also covers suspect identification and interrogation.
2. Present any Output to anyone as real footage, or as a record of a real event, including in news, media or social media.
3. Attempt to depict real, identifiable people, real addresses or locations, or real incidents.
4. Create content that is sexual, involves minors, or is political, religious or communal in nature. Also prohibited: content intended to impersonate, defame, harass, intimidate or deceive.
5. Remove, crop, obscure or alter the visible "SYNTHETIC" marking, invisible watermark or provenance metadata. Attempting to defeat our safeguards is also prohibited. The only exception is clean exports explicitly permitted by a signed enterprise contract.
6. Use the service in breach of any law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules made under it, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 **[VERIFY applicable obligations with counsel]**.

## 5. Labelling and provenance

Outputs carry a visible label, an invisible watermark and signed provenance metadata. These identify the Output as synthetic, along with the generator, the version, the time of creation and your customer ID. Anyone can check an Output on our public verification page. If you share an Output, you must keep its markings intact and identify it as synthetic.

## 6. Clean exports (by exception only)

Where a signed enterprise contract permits it, Outputs may be delivered without the visible label. The watermark, metadata and logging remain in place. You remain fully liable for any onward use, and the restrictions in section 4 still apply in full **[VERIFY legality of this exception with counsel, SR-9]**.

## 7. Logging

We log prompts, parameters, output hashes and the users who requested them, to operate the service, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations. Logs are retained for the period set in our retention policy **[OPEN: period to be set by counsel]**.

## 8. Misuse reporting and enforcement

Anyone can report suspected misuse on the verification page. We may investigate any account, suspend access immediately, and terminate this agreement for breach of section 4. We may also share relevant information with authorities where the law requires it.

## 9. Limits of the safeguards

Watermarks and labels can be degraded, cropped or covered by a determined bad actor. Our safeguards make provenance checkable; they do not make misuse impossible. If verification finds no marks, that does not prove a video is real.
